# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Lowground is a collaborative open-source project. We want participation to be respectful, constructive and welcoming, regardless of experience level, musical background, identity, location or technical opinion.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- give and receive constructive feedback;
- explain technical disagreements with evidence and clarity;
- welcome newcomers and help them find context;
- respect privacy, consent and creative ownership;
- focus discussion on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks;
- sexualized or hateful language directed at another person;
- doxxing, publishing private information or exposing credentials;
- deliberately disrupting rooms, tests, issue trackers or community spaces;
- plagiarism or presenting someone else's work as your own;
- sustained bad-faith behavior after a clear request to stop.

## Project spaces

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, test rooms and any project space used by the Lowground community.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal data or credentials in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation or take other appropriate action.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, privacy and the continued ability of the community to collaborate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards and is intended to support a healthy project, not to replace applicable law or platform rules.
